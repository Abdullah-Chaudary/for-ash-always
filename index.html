TO MY ASH ♡

Happy birthday, my love. ♡

I hope nineteen brings you all the happiness your heart can hold, the kind of peace that feels like home, and countless little moments that make you smile for no reason.

You're the woman who made me believe in love. You're my biggest supporter, my favourite person, and someone I never want to stop choosing. I love your eyes, your smile, your hair, your nature, your little quirks, and every tiny thing that makes you who you are. Honestly, I don't think you realise just how beautiful you are to me.

Sometimes I wish I could skip ahead to a life where I get to wake up next to you every single morning. Where I get to tuck your hair behind your ears, kiss your forehead and your hands before the day begins, pull you into the tightest hugs, and steal little kisses on your nose throughout the day. Yes, that adorable little nose of yours. I don't think I'll ever get tired of kissing it. I want to smell your beautiful hair, hold you close, take care of you, and find a hundred little ways to remind you how loved you are.

I also want to say I'm sorry. I'm sorry for the times I've hurt you, for the moments I've made you feel anything less than loved, and especially for lately, when my own struggles have sometimes stopped me from treating you the way you deserve. I know having things on my mind doesn't make it okay to take anything out on you. You deserve patience, affection, reassurance, and the very best of me. I'm still figuring things out, but I never want you to feel like you're paying the price for the battles I'm fighting.

There's so much I want to achieve in life, and a big part of why I push myself so hard is because I dream of a future with you. I want to build a life where circumstances never get to decide whether we can be together. Where I can stand beside you, make you my wife, take care of you, and give us the life we dream about. I don't want to lose something as precious as you because I wasn't able to build the stability and future I know we deserve. I want to become someone who can turn all these promises into reality.

I want to be the person who shows up for you, who brings you everything you need before you even have to ask, who celebrates your smallest victories and stands beside you through your hardest days. I want to give you the world, put it at your feet, and treat you like the princess you deserve to be. Not just on your birthday, but on ordinary Tuesdays, difficult days, and every day in between.

You deserve a love so beautiful that the whole world would be jealous of the way you're loved. And I hope I get to spend my life giving you exactly that.

Thank you for being you. For making my world softer, brighter, and so much more meaningful. I may not always find the perfect words, but I hope you never have to question how deeply I love you or how grateful I am that it's you.

If I could make one wish today, it would be to have the privilege of waking up to your face for the rest of my life.

Happy nineteenth birthday, my love. I love you, more than these words could ever explain.

Always yours, ♡
